Gunpowder & Grimoires

A fast-paced 2.5D top-down dark fantasy rogue-lite shooter where enchanted firearms collide with arcane grimoires inside procedurally generated dungeons.

⬇ Download Latest Release Source Code on GitHub

Core Features

Built on Godot Engine 4.6.2 with 60Hz physics interpolation, multithreaded dungeon generation, and deep graphics customization.

🕯️

2.5D Dynamic Lighting

Normal-mapped stone walls, dynamic shadows, flickering dungeon lanterns, and full graphics presets from Potato to Ultra.

🗺️

Procedural Dungeons

Every floor is generated on a background CPU thread with chests, monster waves, scaling difficulty, and a dedicated Boss arena.

💀

Boss Fights & Progression

Track the Boss on the minimap, battle through escalating floors with a Boss HP bar, and unlock the portal to descend deeper.

⚙️

Deep Customization

Tabbed settings menu with full key rebinding, customizable damage numbers, FPS overlay, V-Sync, Exclusive Fullscreen, and audio sliders.

Gunpowder & Magic

Switch weapons on the fly and combine firepower with grimoire spells to survive.

🔫 Firearms

  • Pistol — Balanced precision sidearm with reliable damage.
  • Shotgun — 5 piercing pellets with devastating close-range spread.
  • Rifle — High fire-rate automatic weapon for crowd control.

📖 Arcane Grimoires & Loot

  • Heal Spell — Restores health in critical moments (45s cooldown).
  • Random Grimoire — Casts a random Speed Boost, Damage Multiplier, or Arcane Shield (30s cooldown).
  • Dungeon Chests — Guaranteed potions and buffs scattered across the labyrinth.
